Signals used in this estimate

Machine status

Breakdown and maintenance-due statuses raise the score. Inactive machines are listed without an active risk score.

Breakdown pattern

Breakdown records created in the last 180 days add risk. Repeated incidents are flagged as unusual for review.

Maintenance schedule

Overdue or near-term open maintenance jobs raise attention. Suggested dates prioritize high risk for next-day review, watch items within 7 days, and low risk within 30 days.
