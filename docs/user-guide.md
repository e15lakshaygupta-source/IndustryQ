# INDUSTRIQ user guide

This guide covers the day-to-day flows currently available in the application. Your role and organization assignment determine which records and actions appear.

## Sign in and account security

1. Open the INDUSTRIQ URL provided by your administrator and sign in with your assigned username and password.
2. Use **My profile** to update your contact details and change your password.
3. Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page if password recovery has been configured for your deployment. Contact your administrator if no reset email arrives.
4. Sign out using the button at the bottom of the navigation menu, especially on shared devices.

## Find your workspace

The dashboard shows the operational summary available to your role. Use the navigation menu to open Organization, Workforce, Production, Inventory, Billing, Quality & Intelligence, Reports, and Enterprise pages. The dashboard search finds employees, machines, inventory items, and invoices that your account is permitted to see.

The mobile page can be added to a phone or tablet home screen using the browser's install/add-to-home-screen menu. It requires a network connection for account data and actions.

## Common employee tasks

- Open **Employee portal** to review your profile, shifts, attendance, leave requests, payslips, and assigned training.
- Use the attendance punch flow to check in or out. Confirm the date and status on the attendance page.
- Submit a leave request from the leave area and check its status in the same place.
- Complete assigned training and confirm the completion status appears in Training.

## Common manager tasks

- Review attendance, leave requests, shifts, workforce records, and reports within your assigned scope.
- In Production, review plans and work orders and record daily output according to local procedures.
- In Inventory, review stock and procurement activity; use the available movement workflows to record receipts or adjustments.
- In Quality & Intelligence, review inspections, machine status, maintenance, and energy records. Use approval/review actions only when authorized.
- In Billing, create draft invoices or supplier bills, review tax and party details, then use the documented approval/issue workflow. Record payments against the correct document and retain its receipt.

## Working with records

- Use the record's detail page to review its current status, history, and available actions.
- Use search and filters to narrow lists. If an expected record is missing, ask an administrator to confirm your company, factory, department, role, and the record's location.
- Do not share passwords, API keys, invoice PDFs, or private business documents in chat or email without following your organization's approved process.

## Getting help

Use the support contact and escalation route supplied during client handover. Include the page, approximate time, record reference, and steps that led to the issue. Do not send passwords, session cookies, API keys, or unnecessary personal data. For urgent safety or production incidents, follow the site's existing incident procedure first.
